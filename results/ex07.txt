=== Task 4: unbalance dataset ===
Unbalance has a few huge dense clusters and several tiny ones. Maximin crossover from two parents can still under-represent tiny clusters if neither parent placed a centroid there. It *can* work if the pooled set covers both large and small modes; otherwise the child inherits the blind spot.

=== Task 5: outlier detection inside GA crossover ===
Yes — treat parent centroids as a point set and score them (e.g. KDIST / mean-shift). High-score 'outlier centroids' often sit in empty space (useless prototypes) and can be discarded before maximin selection; low-score centroids sit in dense modes and are preferred. Caution: a legitimate centroid of a small/rare cluster can look like an outlier — protect low-SSE / high-mass centroids from removal.
